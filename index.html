Chris Colton

Head Trainer · Baseball

Newnan native drafted by the Seattle Mariners in 2001. Six years in their system and four more in independent ball, with a .335 season in 2005. He’s coached more than 500 players.

J.D. Williams

Instructor · Baseball

Switch-hitting outfielder taken by the Minnesota Twins in the 10th round in 2010. He coaches from both sides of the plate, with a lot of attention on fundamentals and the mental side.

Jackson Sullivan

Pitching Coach · Baseball

Four years on the mound at Erskine College, including a win in the conference championship game, after four varsity years at Trinity Christian. Works on velocity, movement pitches, control and arm care.

Lane Thaxton

Softball Coach

Sixteen years in softball, ten of them pitching, including travel ball with Adidas Show Elite. Hit .322 at Blue Mountain Christian and made SSAC First-Team All-Conference. Teaches hitting, pitching and fielding, ages 8 and up.

Weekly clinics

Tuesdays and
Saturdays.

Small groups, one coach, one skill a week. Come for a single week or sign up for the whole block.

  • Tuesday evening, baseball. Chris runs the group, rotating hitting, pitching and fielding week to week. Jackson follows with a pitching-only clinic.
  • Saturday morning, softball. Lane runs the group on the same rotation, then stays for a softball pitching clinic.
  • Spots are capped so every kid gets real reps. Call or text to hold one.
