Support

If your question is about a booking, send the name on the account and the date of the reservation. It saves a round trip.

Booking

Cages, lessons and clinics are booked in the Batters Boxx app or at battersboxx.com once you have an account. You can book up to 30 days out, and at least an hour before start time. Payment is taken at the counter when you arrive.

Cancelling or changing a booking

Open Your reservations and tap Cancel. You can cancel yourself up to 4 hours before start time. Inside 4 hours, call or text us and we'll sort it out. To move a booking, cancel it and book the new time.

Hour packages

Hours from a package are used automatically when you book a cage. Your account shows how many hours you have left and when the package expires. If you turned on auto-refill and want it off, you can switch it off from your account, or ask us to.

Forgot your password

Call, text or email us from the address on the account and we'll reset it for you.

Deleting your account

Email info@battersboxx.com from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We'll cancel any upcoming bookings and remove your account and its booking history within 7 days, then confirm by email. The privacy policy explains what we keep and why.

Drive Baseball families

Drive Baseball, our travel-ball club, uses the same account system. One login works on both, and you can book Batters Boxx lanes, lessons and clinics from either one.
