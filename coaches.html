Assistant Trainer

JD Williams

JaDamion "J.D." Williams is a switch-hitting outfielder from Odessa, Florida. The Minnesota Twins drafted him in the 10th round (315th overall) in 2010. He coaches from both sides of the plate and puts the emphasis on fundamentals and mental preparation.

Head Trainer

Chris Colton

Former pro outfielder from Newnan. The Seattle Mariners drafted him in the 15th round in 2001 out of Middle Georgia State, and he spent six years in their system (2002-2007), reaching Advanced A before four more seasons of independent ball. His best year was 2005, when he hit .335 / .374 / .581. He's coached more than 500 players.

  • Teaches hitting and infield / outfield and pitching
  • Baseball
  • Here Mon, Tue, Fri

Pitching Coach

Jackson Sullivan

Jackson pitched four years at Erskine College in Due West, South Carolina, where he just finished a degree in Sport Management. As a sophomore he started throughout the developmental tournament and was the winning pitcher in the conference championship game. Before college he pitched four years of varsity at Trinity Christian in Sharpsburg. With younger pitchers he works on velocity, movement pitches, control and arm care.

  • Teaches pitching
  • Baseball
  • Here Tue, Wed
Book with Sullivan

Game Performance Specialist

Joseph Lucas

Joseph is a game performance and mindset coach from Port Charlotte, Florida. He served in the Marine Corps, holds an M.S. in Sports Management from Georgetown, is NESTA-certified in speed and agility, and coaches 9th grade baseball at Newnan High School. He founded ProLevel Performance, where he trains focus, composure and quick decisions under pressure.

Softball Coach

Lane Thaxton

Lane has played softball for 16 years, 10 of them as a pitcher, including travel ball with Adidas Show Elite out of Birmingham, Alabama. At Blue Mountain Christian University she hit .322 with 7 home runs and 27 RBI and made SSAC First-Team All-Conference. She's given private lessons for two years and spent about three as an assistant coach with the Firecracker organization. She works on confidence, skills and softball knowledge, and on building a strong work ethic on and off the field.

  • Teaches pitching and hitting and infield / outfield
  • Softball
