How it works

Book the
block.

Teams rent the whole facility, not single cages. Pick a length and a start time where the building is empty, then split the roster across the lanes and rotate on your own whistle.

While your block is on the board, nobody else can book a cage or a lesson. It's one flat hourly rate.
